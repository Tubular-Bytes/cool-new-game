## Player-driven market #proposed

Raised as a wanted layer, not yet designed.

## Key open decision #open

**Single global market vs. regionalized (per-planet/proximity) market.** This cascades into
[[Territory & Logistics]]:

- A single global auction house is frictionless and easy for new players, but sits apart from the
  distance-matters theme the tile/logistics system is building.
- A regionalized market — price/availability varies by planet, arbitrage means physically shipping goods
  subject to the same strain mechanics as everything else — ties trade into the spatial economy instead of
  floating above it. Better thematic fit given the interplanetary strain system, but adds friction for new
  players.

No decision made yet; noted as something to settle in the same pass as the logistics-strain curve.
