## Two resolution layers #decided

- **Hourly tick** — production, movement, battles.
- **Daily cutoff** — political and economic decisions, resolved globally at a set time.

## Open design tension #open

If everything meaningful (combat) resolves hourly and politics/economy only resolves daily, players may
perceive the daily layer as pure bookkeeping and the hourly layer as "the real game."

Mitigation direction: at least one daily-cadence lever should meaningfully affect the next 24 hourly ticks —
e.g. a policy decision that shifts production ratios or defensive bonuses until the next daily cutoff. Not yet
designed which specific levers do this.

## Splits & the daily cadence

[[Splits & Secession]] negotiation happens within the daily cadence, but is explicitly **not forced** to a
fixed number of cycles — see that page for the "resolve now vs keep talking" mechanic.
