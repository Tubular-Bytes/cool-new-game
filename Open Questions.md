Unresolved items, roughly in the order they'd block further design. Use this as the agenda for the next
session.

## Splits & Secession (most developed, closest to locked)
- Can the losing side of a loyalty-based fallback allocation escalate to open conflict anyway, or is
  escalation reserved for over-threshold contested overlap only?
- What's the exact contested-tile percentage threshold that triggers escalation vs. fallback allocation?
- What's the exact claim-budget formula (how founding stake, pop count, and loyalty are weighted)?
- What's the cost/cooldown to *declare* a split, to stop constant fragmentation of large collectives?
→ [[Splits & Secession]]

## Collectives
- Do different collective "forms" (federation, syndicate, commune, bloc) get concrete mechanical stat
  deltas, or does this stay purely narrative for now?
→ [[Collectives]]

## Population & Growth
- Do pops carry attributes beyond loyalty (skills, an origin tag), or is loyalty-per-tile the whole model?
→ [[Population & Growth]]

## Territory & Logistics
- What's the actual formula/curve for interplanetary logistics strain?
- What's the UI treatment for surfacing strain before a player overextends?
→ [[Territory & Logistics]]

## Diplomacy
- Which (if any) of the three proposed mechanics — public treaty registry, alliance intelligence-sharing,
  reputation cost for breaking treaties — actually gets built, and how does it interact with the
  [[Splits & Secession]] conflict system?
→ [[Diplomacy]]

## Market
- Single global market, or regionalized by planet/proximity (tied to logistics strain)?
→ [[Market]]

## Not yet discussed at all
- What does "loss" look like — can a collective be wiped out entirely, or is there always a smallest viable
  unit that keeps a player in the game?
- Session shape: how often is a player expected to check in, and how much of the daily-cadence layer is
  meant to be handled in a couple of minutes vs. requiring real thought?
- What tiered-cadence lever (beyond splits) gives the daily layer real teeth over the hourly combat/production
  layer, so it doesn't read as pure bookkeeping? → [[Tick Cadence]]
