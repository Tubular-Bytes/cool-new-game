## Base unit #decided

Players control a **collective** from the start, not a "nation" — the term is deliberately vague so the game
can later explore political-organization themes where "nation" isn't the right descriptor.
Collectives are made up of pops. Eventually these pops might specialize but at this time there’s only a generic pop type.

## Economy #decided

The economy is micro-managed, controlled by buildings and allocated pops. Buildings try to use materials and create products (or use intermediary products to create products). Pops can be assigned manually to control priorities or removed completely to turn the building off.

## Possible forms #proposed

Idea (from early discussion, not yet locked): let a collective's internal structure be a real game object,
not just a label. Different forms could carry different mechanical strengths/weaknesses:

- **Federation** — pools production across member groups, but slower to declare war
- **Corporate syndicate** — efficient trade, weaker territorial claims
- **Religious commune** — cohesion/loyalty bonus, other trade-offs TBD
- **Militarized bloc** — combat-oriented, other trade-offs TBD

Status: directional only — no mechanical stat deltas defined yet. See [[Open Questions]].
